# FL-INS-005 — NFIP / flood semantics

Allowed wording: **“Listed in FEMA/NFIP Agency Registry.”**

Forbidden without separate proof: “NFIP certified,” “NFIP-certified agent,” “Flood-certified.”

Registry listing ≠ FIRA training completion. OIR states agencies may be in the registry database and still omitted from the quoting tool until FIRA is marked yes.

## Grain

Enrollment collects **agency NPN** and **principal agent NPN**. The public agencies list shows agency + a named person, **without NPN**. Do not attach person↔agency by name.

Exact NPN only. Public list rows remain UNRESOLVED / held.

## Flood market boundary

Do not add together:

- NFIP registry participation
- private flood insurance (admitted)
- surplus-lines flood activity
- any Citizens flood-related context

as one “flood insurer” count.
