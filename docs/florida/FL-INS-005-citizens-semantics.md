# FL-INS-005 — Citizens semantics

Citizens Property Insurance Corporation is **Florida residual-market infrastructure**.

Not automatically “Florida’s largest insurer.” Any size claim needs a **dated official** extract.

Citizens participation ≠ general Florida licensure. Citizens agent authorization ≠ DFS license. Do not replace DFS credentials.

## Policy count

Every PIF must be dated. Do not reuse secondary/board/press numbers. Policies **in force** ≠ policies **serviced** after depopulation.

## Takeout / depopulation

An OIR consent order or calendar slot that **authorizes an offer** is not a completed assumption. Attach takeout companies only with exact NAIC / mapped Florida Company Code.

## Geography

Only from Citizens/OIR official market files. Not from agency mailing addresses or DFS county appointments.
