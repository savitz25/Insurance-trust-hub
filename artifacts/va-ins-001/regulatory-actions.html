Regulatory Actions

Notify me of updates to this page

The State Corporation Commission is authorized to examine and investigate insurance institutions under §§ 38.2-1317.1 and 38.2-200 of the Code of Virginia. Regulatory actions are investigations that may be prompted by:

  • market analysis (§ 38.2-1317.2)
  • consumer complaints
  • an insurance company’s failure to follow its rate or form filings
  • general business practice violations identified during a market conduct examination 
  • an insurer’s internal compliance audit
