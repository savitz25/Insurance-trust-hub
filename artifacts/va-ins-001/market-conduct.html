Market Conduct Examination Reports

Notify me of updates to this page

The State Corporation Commission is authorized to examine and investigate insurance institutions under §§ 38.2-1317.1 and 38.2-200 of the Code of Virginia.

The Market Conduct staff performs field and desk examinations on Property and Casualty (P&C) and Life and Health (L&H) insurers. The scope and conduct of the examinations, to the extent practicable, are in accordance with the guidelines and procedures set forth in the Market Regulation Handbook and such other guidelines and procedures as the Commission deems appropriate.
